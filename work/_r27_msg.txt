P1 the evidence identity was still caller-supplied
- The factory already rejected raw ResearchEvidenceRef(...), but it accepted a
  caller-named source_id. That meant one real reading could be renamed into
  FACT_A / FACT_B / FACT_C, defeating the (source_type, source_id, as_of)
  duplicate and conflict identity. s_of was derived, identity was not.
- vidence_ref_from_market_reading no longer takes source_id at all. Identity
  is derived from the R24 projection as policy @ observed_at; a missing policy
  or a missing observation stamp now fails closed instead of being patched by the
  caller.
- Honest renaming: the previous round called this boundary "owner-issued
  provenance", which overclaimed. If a thing's identity can be forged, and three
  lines of a test helper are already doing it, the label is the bug.
  MarketDataReading / MarketDataSnapshot are public dataclasses, so two-step
  forgery is reachable:

      hand-built MarketDataSnapshot(verified, cross_source)
        -> hand-built MarketDataReading
        -> evidence_ref_from_market_reading(...)
        -> a ref with verification=verified

  The module and ARCHITECTURE.md now state what this layer does and does not
  guarantee, and AI-TYPED-06 asserts the two-step path as a *documented
  limitation* rather than pretending it is closed. Real provenance needs R24 to
  issue an evidence token; that is R24's job, deferred and listed as such.
  No issued=True, no importable sentinel, no factory registry.
