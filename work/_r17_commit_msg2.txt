fix(risk): resolve archived entry signals by exact id

Codex review of head 8071253b found a real gap in the new episode provenance:
a sliced entry leaves its originating signal at `deferred_capacity` after the
first slice fills, while the order is already filled and execution-verified.
`_cleanup_stale_data` later moves that row into `paper_signals_archive` (same
columns, same id) and deletes it from `paper_signals`, so the exact-id lookup
reported `signal_not_found` for an otherwise intact chain and the episode's real
model score silently degraded to the neutral 50.

The exact-id lookup now covers both tables with identical identity and as-of
checks and reports the row source; the archive is only ever read as
`WHERE id=?` (statically enforced), so this is still exact provenance and never
a latest-search fallback.

New: RP-15, RP-16, RP-16b, production regression RISK-REVIEW-P10 (drives the
real `_cleanup_stale_data`), mutations M-PR14 and M-PR15 (15/15 RED).
